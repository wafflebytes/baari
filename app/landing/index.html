Questions families ask.

Does our cook need a new app?

Just Telegram. She hears voice notes and replies by voice. She never types and never pays for anything.

Do my parents have to use the app?

No. Whoever sets things up uses the app. Parents do everything on Telegram.

How does Baari spend our money?

Only inside limits you set: a monthly block, a per-payment limit and a daily cap, and only to shops on your list. Bigger bills go to a parent as a Pine Labs link.

What happens if the groceries are late?

Baari checks at 10:45 pm and again at 6:30 am. If it's late, it books a rider, moves things to the kirana, or cooks the runner-up.

What does Baari know about our health?

Only plate rules, like no aloo for Papa. It never asks about a medical condition or names one.

Is this live?

It's a working build from The Ken and Pine Labs buildathon. Telegram, Gnani voice and Pine Labs links are real, with Pine Labs on its sandbox. Delhivery and the small-payment block are mocks of their documented APIs.
