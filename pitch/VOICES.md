# Three voices

The script in `pitch/SCRIPT.md` is written in three voices. Each has a skill in `.claude/skills/`, so any of our Claude Code sessions can rewrite a line in the right person's voice.

| Speaker | Skill | Status | Sections |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Chaitanya | `chaitanya-demo-voice` | His own file, copied in on 10 Oct | Everything except Vinay's night and wiring |
| Vinay | `vinay-demo-voice` | His own file, installed 10 Oct | One night, and how it's wired |
| Keshav | `keshav-demo-voice` | Not speaking at the finale (10 Oct); his sections went to Chaitanya | none |

How the three sound next to each other:
- **Chaitanya** has the opinions. A real moment, then a plain claim said with conviction. "Why should it bother you otherwise?"
- **Keshav** has the evidence. A quote from a real family, a pause, then what it means. He's the one who admits what we got wrong.
- **Vinay** has the mechanism. Times, numbers and what the system actually did, said fast and dry. He names the part before he starts, asks the audience's question and answers it with "so", and closes with "So yeah, this was all about...".

## Rewriting a section

In a Claude Code session in this repo, for example:

```text
/keshav-demo-voice rewrite the "What we heard" section of pitch/SCRIPT.md. Keep every fact and the word count.
```

Keep each section's word count. The script runs about 7:40 and the slot is 8:00.
