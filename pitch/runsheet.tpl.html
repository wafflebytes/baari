The first 100 homes

Paul Graham's rule: do things that don't scale. Recruit by hand, one house at a time, and treat the first ten as if they were the only ten.

Weeks 3 to 410Our own homes and our neighbours'. Set up in the kitchen, with the cook there for her first voice note.
Weeks 5 to 830One housing society. A resident posts in its WhatsApp group, never us. A weekend table at the gate: bring your phone, ten minutes.
Weeks 9 to 12100Three more societies where a stage-2 cook also works, or a stage-2 family's relatives live. Plus the reel's creator, and whoever she brings.

Before the first home

Weeks 1 and 2. Households on rails (today it runs one), a run lock per household, and the cost of one night measured on our own homes.

The gate

Six of the first ten homes still run a night in week three without a reminder. If not, stop recruiting and fix what made them stop.

A human reads every night

For 30 days, one of us reads every home's decision log each morning and calls when something looks wrong. A hundred logs at two minutes is about three hours a day across three of us.

The number on the wall

In week four, someone other than Mummy holds the baari three nights a week.

Grilled: what the first draft got wrong

  • "Set it up for the 14 families we interviewed."Three of the six transcripts say "no cook". They told us the problem; they aren't homes where a cook needs a brief. Start with our own homes and neighbours.
  • "The parent's first payment makes it real."No real money yet: Pine Labs is sandbox, and live needs a merchant account and a company. The first hundred pay the kirana by UPI as today. Real money is part of the ask to Pine Labs.
  • "Parents stay on Telegram."Parents live on WhatsApp. For a hundred homes we install Telegram on their phones during setup. WhatsApp is what the next thousand need, and it isn't built.
  • "Reply to all 54 commenters."Most comments are tags and laughing emojis, and a new account replying gets flagged. Write to one person: the creator. She has a cook and 114K viewers.
  • "Win one cook, get her whole route."A cook may not want employers comparing notes. Neighbours recruit, through the society group. The cook mentions it because she benefits: a clear brief and groceries already paid.
  • "Run it all on AgenticOrg."Unknown: concurrent run limits and cost. A hundred homes is about 1,200 agent runs a night. Measure in stage 1; be ready to run the agent ourselves.
