## BAARI routine: Sharma household day
Household: Sharma, Flat 402. 20:30 the turn-holder gets two dishes to pick from; everyone else sends a wish. The dish locks as soon as the holder picks, or at 21:30 from the wishes. The baari moves to the next person. 22:45 and 06:30 delivery checks. 07:40 Sunita at Sharma Kirana. 07:45 Hindi voice brief to Sunita. 08:00 Sunita arrives. 08:05 her reply is read. 08:20 Vinay told if she hasn't replied.
