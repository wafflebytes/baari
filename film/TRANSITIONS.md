# Transitions

One row per cut in the three videos: when it happens, what carries it, why it's there, the word that lands on it, and the Base44 move it copies (`film/LOOK.md`, "Motion"; strips in `film/trailer/look-ref/`). Times are seconds in the cut. Trailer times follow `film/trailer/timing.json` (189 beats at 128 BPM, 88.6 s); if the lead moves a shot, the times here move with it.

A carrier is the thing that connects the two sides of a cut: an object that stays put or turns into the next shot, the island, a word, or the beat itself. "Beat" as a carrier means a hard cut on the grid with nothing held across it, which is what Base44 does for most of its cuts.
